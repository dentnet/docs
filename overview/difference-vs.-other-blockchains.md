# Difference vs. Other Blockchains

DENTNet is a special-purpose blockchain for [telecommunication-assets](advanced-concepts/telecommunication-assets/ "mention"). The architecture and concepts of DENTNet allow mobile operators to do parts of their operations on a blockchain.&#x20;

With our concept of [operator-sponsorship.md](advanced-concepts/operator-sponsorship.md "mention") it is possible to create wallets where fees are paid by an operator. This way, mobile operators provide native wallets to their subscribers, either custodial or non-custodial.

Customers purchasing mobile assets want to use them. They do phone calls or use mobile data and their remaining balance gets deducted. DENTNet implements [telco-asset-usage.md](advanced-concepts/telco-asset-usage.md "mention"), that enables usage tracking of asset balances on-chain.&#x20;

Mobile Operators are selling assets, like mobile data packages, that are expiring. Unlike other blockchain protocols, DENTNet has an [telco-asset-expiry.md](advanced-concepts/telco-asset-expiry.md "mention") management build-in to reflect these market-specific rules.
