# Buy on DENTNet

You can purchase DENTX directly on the DENTNet Dashboard.

Check the list for supported payment methods:

* [Binance Pay](https://pay.binance.com/en)

Follow these steps to buy DENTX on the DENTNet Dashboard:

1. [Create an account on DENTNet](buy-on-dentnet.md#id-1.-create-dentnet-account)
2. [Connect your wallet on DENTNet Dashboard](buy-on-dentnet.md#id-2.-connect-wallet-to-dashboard)
3. [Buy DENTX ](buy-on-dentnet.md#id-3.-buy-dentx)

#### 1. Create DENTNet account

To create an account, use one of the supported [Wallets](https://docs.dentnet.io/dentnet-documentation/tools/wallets).

#### 2. Connect wallet to Dashboard

Use your DENTNet wallet to connect it to the Dashboard as described in the [Connect Wallet ](../../tools/dashboard.md#connect-wallet)section.

#### 3. Buy DENTX

Go to the [Buy](https://main.dentnet.io/dashboard/#/buy) section of the DENTNet Dashboard and enter either the amount you want to pay or the amount of DENTX you want to receive.

Review the fees and the total amount to pay. Once you've done that, click the Binance Pay button to proceed. You will be prompted to authenticate the transaction using your wallet.

After that, you can continue the process on the Binance Pay portal. Your DENTX balance will be automatically updated once the payment has been successfully processed.
