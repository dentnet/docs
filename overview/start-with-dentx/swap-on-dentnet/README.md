# Swap on DENTNet

DENTNet offers swapping DENT tokens for DENTX on DENTNet. To use your DENTs on Ethereum to get DENTX on DENTNet, you need to follow these steps:

1. [Create an account on DENTNet](./#id-1.-create-dentnet-account)
2. [Bridge DENT tokens from your Ethereum wallet](./#id-2.-bridge-dent-token-from-an-ethereum-wallet)
3. [Swap DENT to DENTX on DENTNet](./#id-3.-swap-dents-to-dentx)

#### 1. Create DENTNet account

To create an account, use one of the supported [wallets](../../../tools/wallets/ "mention").

#### 2. Bridge DENT Token from your Ethereum wallet

If you already have DENT on Ethereum, e.g., in a MEW or Metamask wallet, you can use the [bridge](../../../tools/bridge/ "mention") to transfer the Ethereum DENTs to your DENTNet account.

Follow the instructions in the [move-dents-from-ethereum-to-dentnet.md](../../../tools/bridge/move-dents-from-ethereum-to-dentnet.md "mention") section.

#### 3. Swap DENTs to DENTX

Swap the DENT token you received in the wallet to DENTX as described in the [swap-dent-on-dentnet.md](swap-dent-on-dentnet.md "mention") section using the [#dent-to-dentx-section](../../../tools/dashboard.md#dent-to-dentx-section "mention")of the DENTNet Dashboard.

Be aware, that only for the initial Swap from DENT token to DENTX, the fee is paid in DENT token. Once there is any DENTX on the wallet, all fees are paid in DENTX.&#x20;

Make sure to leave enough DENTX on your account for further swaps or staking payouts.
