# Move DENTX from Ethereum to DENTNet

Follow these steps to move your DENTX from Ethereum to DENTNet:

### **1. Secure Your DENTX in a Smart Contract-Compatible Wallet:**

* Ensure your DENTX tokens are stored in an Ethereum wallet that supports smart contracts, such as a non-custodial wallet like Metamask.

### **2. Connect Your Wallet on DENT Bridge:**

* Visit the DENT Bridge website at [https://main.dentnet.io/bridge/](https://main.dentnet.io/bridge/) to begin the transition process.
* Click "Connect" and select "Use Ethereum Wallet".
* Follow the on-screen instructions to connect your non-custodial wallet to the DENT Bridge.

## **3. Send Your DENTX to a DENTNet Address:**

* Once your wallet is connected to DENT Bridge, select "DENTNet" as the destination network. Select "**DENTX**" as the token to be transferred.
* Enter the amount of DENTX you wish to send to the DENTNet.
* Input the DENTNet address where you want to deposit your tokens by entering the account address.
* Double-check the accuracy of the address to avoid any errors.

DENTNet addresses always start with **dx.**

## **4. Transfer to DENTNet wallet - Confirm Transaction on Explorer:**

* The DENTX will be deposited into your DENTNet wallet
* Open the [explorer.md](../explorer.md "mention") and enter the account address to view the transaction and your updated DENT balance.
* Please note that the transaction may take a few minutes to complete, so be patient.
* If you're unable to see your DENTX after 20 minutes, contact the support team for assistance.

By following these steps, you can securely transfer your DENTX token from the Ethereum network to the DENTNet.
