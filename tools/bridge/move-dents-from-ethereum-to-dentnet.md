# Move DENTs from Ethereum to DENTNet

Follow these steps to move your DENT tokens to DENTNet:

### **1. Secure Your DENTs in a Smart Contract-Compatible Wallet:**

* Ensure your DENT tokens are stored in a wallet that supports smart contracts, such as a non-custodial wallet like Metamask.
* If needed, transfer your DENT tokens to your non-custodial wallet.

### **2. Connect Your Wallet on DENT Bridge:**

* Visit the DENT Bridge website at [https://main.dentnet.io/bridge/](https://main.dentnet.io/bridge/) to begin the transition process.
* Click "Connect" and select "Use Ethereum Wallet".
* Follow the on-screen instructions to connect your non-custodial wallet to the DENT Bridge.

### **3. Send Your DENTs to a DENTNet Address:**

* Once your wallet is connected to DENT Bridge, select "DENTNet" as the destination network. Select "DENT" as the token to be transferred.
* Enter the amount of DENT tokens you wish to send to the DENTNet.
* Input the DENTNet address where you want to deposit your tokens.&#x20;
  * If you want to send your DENT tokens **to your DENT App account**, you can find your DENTNet wallet address in the DENT app by navigating to Account -> Deposits.
  * If you **created an account on DENTNet**, e.g. using the [vault-app.md](../wallets/vault-app.md "mention"), you want to send your DENT tokens by entering the account address.
* Double-check the accuracy of the address to avoid any errors.

{% hint style="warning" %}
DENTNet addresses always start with **dx.**
{% endhint %}

### **4a. Transfer to DENT App - Confirm Transaction on the DENT App:**

* The DENT tokens will be deposited into your DENTNet wallet within the DENT app.
* Open the DENT app to view your updated DENT balance.
* Please note that the transaction may take a few minutes to complete, so be patient.
* If you're unable to see your DENT tokens after 20 minutes, contact the support team for assistance.

### **4b. Transfer to DENTNet wallet - Confirm Transaction on Explorer:**

* The DENT tokens will be deposited into your DENTNet wallet
* Open the [explorer.md](../explorer.md "mention") and enter the account address to view the transaction and your updated DENT balance.
* Please note that the transaction may take a few minutes to complete, so be patient.
* If you're unable to see your DENT tokens after 20 minutes, contact the support team for assistance.

By following these steps, you can securely transfer your DENT tokens from the Ethereum network to the DENTNet, ensuring continued accessibility and usability within the DENT ecosystem.
