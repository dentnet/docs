# Explorer

[https://main.dentnet.io/explorer/](Link to DENTNet Explorer)

DENTNet Explorer is a blockchain explorer that allows users to interact with and explore the contents of the DENTNet blockchain. Similar to etherscan, a DENTNet explorer provides detailed information about transactions, blocks, and addresses on the blockchain. Understanding how to use DENTNet Explorer effectively helps track transactions, verify payments, and explore the activity on the blockchain. Here's a brief guide on how to use DENTNet Explorer, focusing on key elements such as extrinsic hash, account address, block hash, and block height:

## **1. Extrinsic Hash:**

* **What is it?** An extrinsic hash, also known as a transaction hash, is a unique identifier assigned to each transaction on the blockchain. Please read the [transactions.md](../overview/core-concepts/transactions.md "mention") section of DENTNet documentation as a reference.&#x20;
* **Why is it helpful?** Extrinsic hashes are crucial for tracking the status and details of individual transactions. They provide a way to uniquely identify and reference a particular transaction within the blockchain's history.
* **How to search:** To search for a specific transaction using its extrinsic hash, simply input the hash into the search bar of DENTNet Explorer and initiate the search.
* **Information provided:** The search results will include details such as the sender and recipient addresses, transaction amount, gas fees, timestamp, and any additional data associated with the transaction.

## **2. Account Address:**

* **What is it?** An account address is a unique identifier associated with a user's account or wallet on the blockchain. Please read the [accounts.md](../overview/core-concepts/accounts.md "mention")section for reference.&#x20;
* **Why is it helpful?** Account addresses allow users to track their transactions, monitor balances, and verify the ownership of assets on the blockchain. They serve as the primary means of identifying and distinguishing between different participants in the network.
* **How to search:** To search for information related to a specific account address, enter the address into the search bar of the blockchain explorer and initiate the search.
* **Information provided:** The search results will display the account's funds in DENTX and DENT; transaction history, including incoming and outgoing transactions, and other relevant activity.

## **3. Block Hash:**

* **What is it?** A block hash is a unique identifier assigned to each block on DENTNet. It serves as a cryptographic fingerprint that uniquely identifies a specific block and its contents.
* **Why is it helpful?** Block hashes enable users to verify the integrity and immutability of the blockchain by providing a way to reference and validate individual blocks in the chain.
* **How to search:** To search for a specific block using its hash, input the hash into the search bar of the DENTNet Explorer and initiate the search.
* **Information provided:** The search results will include details such as the block's timestamp, block height, list of transactions included in the block, miner rewards, and other metadata associated with the block.

## **4. Block Height:**

* **What is it?** Block height refers to the sequential number assigned to each block in DENTNet, indicating its position within the chain. The genesis block has a height of 0, and subsequent blocks are numbered sequentially.
* **Why is it helpful?** Block height provides a convenient way to reference and navigate through the blockchain's history. It allows users to track the chronological order of blocks and measure the progress of DENTNet over time.
* **How to search:** To search for information related to a specific block height, input the height value into the search bar of DENTNet Explorer and initiate the search.
* **Information provided:** The search results will typically display details about the block at the specified height, including its hash, timestamp, list of transactions, and other relevant data.

By utilizing these key elements—extrinsic hash, account address, block hash, and block height—users of DENTNet Explorer can gain valuable insights into the activity and history of the blockchain. Whether tracking transactions, verifying payments, or exploring the blockchain's contents, DENTNet Explorer serves as an indispensable tool for navigating the decentralized world of cryptocurrencies and blockchain technology.
